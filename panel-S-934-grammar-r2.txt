### Axiom Checks

**Prelude speaks the Rust protocol** — PASS. `Annotation::Quote` is a Rust-level sentinel
produced by `expression_to_annotation` in the parser. The `is_expr_annotation` predicate
is a Rust function. The quoting mechanism is defined at the Rust level; the prelude implements
the protocol it exposes, not the other way around.

**No fast paths, no fallbacks, no backwards compatibility** — PASS. No new fast paths or
fallbacks introduced by the round 1 fixes. Changes are doc corrections and corpus tests only
(plus the `///` → `//` nit in parser.rs).

**Correctness, not performance** — PASS. No performance tradeoffs in the grammar/spec domain.

**Loader/prelude agnosticism** — PASS. `@Expr` → `Annotation::Quote` conversion is
parser-level, independent of what the loader or prelude defines. The annotation-based narrowing
mechanism (`@[narrows: T]`, `@[is: T]`) works with any user-defined predicate function, with no
prelude names hardcoded in Rust.

**General case, not specific** — PASS. `expression_to_annotation` handles the general case
(VarRef, annotated VarRef, Dict, implied Call, fallback). The `@Expr` special case is documented
as a reserved sentinel, which is a language-level decision, not a specific-caller accommodation.
